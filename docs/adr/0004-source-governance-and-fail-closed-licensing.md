# ADR 0004: Source Governance and Fail-Closed Licensing

## Decision

Represent candidate legal sources as typed, offline `SourceRecord` metadata and require explicit evidence for every positive permission. Missing or conflicting rights remain unresolved and cannot yield an approved decision.

## Context

Legal datasets combine paper, code, annotations, case records, and legislation with different owners and privacy risks. Public availability and academic publication do not establish redistribution or automated-access rights.

## Consequences

Phase 1 produces a reviewable registry and evidence trail without downloading source content. Future data work must resolve manual actions and preserve attribution, provenance, privacy controls, and applicable licence conditions before use.
