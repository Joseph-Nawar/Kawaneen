# Phase 01 — Legal Data-Source and Licensing Audit

## Status

Phase 1 establishes source governance only. It does not download datasets or implement ingestion, parsing, OCR, NLP, retrieval, APIs, or models.

## Included

- Typed source permissions, licence status, roles, authority, privacy risk, and decisions.
- Offline CSV loading, duplicate detection, cross-field fail-closed validation, and summaries.
- CLI validation and text/JSON summaries.
- Evidence records for 12 requested candidate sources.
- Governance policy, ADR, data-manifest guidance, and an audit report.

## Decision policy

No source is approved merely because it is public, downloadable, official, or described in a paper. The registry distinguishes paper, code, dataset, and original-source rights, and records manual actions for unresolved evidence.

## Exit evidence

The Phase 1 report records the evidence hierarchy, decisions, privacy findings, proposed corpus/evaluation strategy, commands, tests, coverage, and remaining manual actions.
