# Egyptian Court of Cassation

Canonical pages: [Court site](https://cc.gov.eg/) and exact [Terms of Use](https://cc.gov.eg/terms), last updated May 2026; inspected 2026-08-06.

The terms permit legal, academic, research, and documentary viewing/copying, require subscription access, prohibit commercial or unapproved publication, and prohibit unlawful extraction. They prove limited research access conditions only; they do not approve automated acquisition, public redistribution, or public-demo use. Privacy risk is high. Decision: `local_research_only`; manual written permission is required for automation or public quotation.
