# ALARB

## Canonical identity

- Dataset card: [THIQAH-RD/ALARB](https://huggingface.co/datasets/THIQAH-RD/ALARB).
- Paper: [ACL Anthology record](https://aclanthology.org/2025.arabicnlp-main.32/) / DOI `10.18653/v1/2025.arabicnlp-main.32`.
- Pages inspected: the Hugging Face card and the ACL paper, verified 2026-08-06.

## Evidence and limits

The card declares Apache-2.0, Arabic, Parquet, and exact splits of 12,012 train and 1,329 test records. The paper describes 13,341 Saudi commercial cases and verdict prediction, reasoning completion, and regulation-identification tasks. This proves the dataset's published technical description and declared dataset licence. It does not prove permission from the courts whose records underlie the benchmark, privacy mitigation, or public-demo rights.

Technical record: 13,341 records, Arabic, Parquet, case unit, evaluation/benchmark role. Privacy is high and original-record rights are unknown. Decision: `evaluation_only`; no data downloaded.
