# Saudi 9,699-case corpus

## Canonical identity

- Paper: [MDPI Data 11(7), 154](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/11/7/154), DOI `10.3390/data11070154`.
- Exact repository: [0marTarek/Arabic-legal-Summarization-and-classification](https://github.com/0marTarek/Arabic-legal-Summarization-and-classification).
- Pages inspected: MDPI paper record and exact GitHub repository, verified 2026-08-06.

The paper proves 9,699 Saudi Board of Grievances cases, full decisions paired with human abstractive summaries, and Administrative, Commercial, and Criminal categories. The recorded split is 7,759 train and 1,940 test, with category counts of Administrative 6,727, Commercial 2,035, and Criminal 937. The repository has no verified dataset licence.
