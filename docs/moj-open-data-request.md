# Saudi MOJ machine-readable open-data request template

Subject: Request for machine-readable article-level Saudi legislation export and reuse terms

Dear Saudi Ministry of Justice,

Kawaneen requests a machine-readable, article-level export of the selected Saudi legislation collection for legal-information research. For each law and article, please provide:

- stable identifier;
- official title and article number;
- article text;
- law type;
- issuing authority;
- publication and effective dates;
- current status;
- amendment history or latest amendment;
- canonical official source URL.

Please also clarify, in writing, whether the export permits automated access and processing, local indexing, model training, quotation, redistribution, and public demonstration. Please identify attribution requirements, preservation of notices, privacy restrictions, rate limits, update/version policy, and whether the licence covers the underlying official text rather than only a portal interface or derived metadata.

Kawaneen will keep any received material outside version control and will record only sanitized provenance, hashes, schemas, counts, and permissions in its repository. The project will not scrape or bulk-download the portal without an authorized machine-readable route.
